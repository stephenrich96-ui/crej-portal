## Getting Started

### Step 1: Fill Out Access Forms

Fill out the following forms and email them to **usteps@utah.gov** using your CREJ email:

- [0-2 DHHS Private Support Coordinator USTEPS Access Form.pdf](/Desktop/CREJ/DSPD%20files%20needed%20to%20be%20sent/0-2%20DHHS%20Private%20Support%20Coordiantor%20USTEPS%20Access%20Form.pdf)
- [0-8 UPI ACCESS Form.pdf](/Desktop/CREJ/DSPD%20files%20needed%20to%20be%20sent/0-8%20UPI%20ACCESS%20Form.pdf)

**Note:** You can create a Utah ID account [here](https://id.utah.gov/signup).

### Step 2: Access the Utah Learning Portal (ULP)

Access the ULP through this link: [Utah Learning Portal](https://utahlearningportal.com)

- Your username is your email address on file with the division
- ULP will send a default password for your first login
- **Change your password after first login** (see instructions below)

### Step 3: Complete Required Trainings

All required trainings are assigned automatically through the ULP. You'll receive email notifications when trainings are assigned. You have **60 days** to complete each required training (except Medicaid 101, which has a 30-day deadline).

## Required Support Coordinator Core Trainings

### Initial Course Assignments (Due in 60 Days)

#### DSPD SCE: Acquiring and maintaining integrated community-based housing
- **Length:** 25 minutes
- **Frequency:** Every year
- **Description:** Introduces the benefits of integrated, home and community-based housing and provides practical guidance on supporting clients in acquiring and maintaining safe, stable housing within the community.
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

#### DSPD SCE: Profound and complex disabilities (Open Future Learning)
- **Length:** 2 hours 35 minutes
- **Frequency:** Once
- **Description:** Helps participants better understand and support people with profound and complex disabilities. Emphasizes seeing the whole person, recognizing that communication happens in every part of life, and supporting people to have power and control in their daily choices.
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

#### DSPD SCE: Ethics training for support coordinators
- **Length:** 25 minutes
- **Frequency:** Every year
- **Description:** Reviews the ethical standards essential to support coordination. Equips support coordinators with the knowledge and skills to apply ethical principles in decision-making processes.
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

#### DSPD SCE: Finance
- **Length:** 40 minutes
- **Frequency:** Every year
- **Description:** Provides support coordinators with an overview of their role as contracted Medicaid Providers in the payment process, along with guidance on meeting requirements for contracts, licensing, and Medicaid policy and billing. Includes training related to HB125.
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

#### DSPD SCE: Records management
- **Length:** 20 minutes
- **Frequency:** Every year
- **Description:** Guides participants through the proper use of the 6 DSPD HIPAA forms, emphasizing respect for client confidentiality and the appropriate timing for destroying confidential records. Includes navigation of the imaging module in USTEPS.
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

#### DSPD SCE: Health monitoring: The fatal five (Open Future Learning)
- **Length:** 2 hours
- **Frequency:** Once
- **Description:** Covers the "Fatal Five" preventable conditions–aspiration, dehydration, constipation, seizures, and sepsis–that pose health risks to the people you support. Explores the key causes, signs, and symptoms of each condition.
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

### Required Course Assignments (Due by June 5, 2026)

#### Supporting a vision for employment
- **Length:** 5 hours 30 minutes
- **Frequency:** Once
- **Description:** Developed by the State Employment Leadership Network (SELN), this training highlights the vital role case managers play in supporting people to pursue and maintain competitive, integrated employment. Focuses on essential components of effective employment support, presents real-world scenarios, and includes interactive exercises.
- **Access:** [Register for SELN Training](https://seln.org/training/supporting-a-vision-for-employment)
- **Important:** Use the same email address that you use to login to USTEPS and ULP
- **Registration:** Opens one week before course start date and closes one week before course ends
- **Remaining Course Offerings:**
  - **WINTER SECTION:** January 5 – February 13, 2026
  - **SPRING SECTION:** March 2 – April 10, 2026
  - **SUMMER SECTION:** April 27 – June 5, 2026

### More Required Courses (Coming in FY26)

#### DSPD SCE: Level of care and Medicaid eligibility
- **Length:** 45 minutes
- **Frequency:** Once
- **Description:** Provides an overview of the two-step waiver Medicaid determination process, covering both level of care and waiver Medicaid eligibility. Learn how to navigate the waiver review process and identify and report changes that may affect eligibility.
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

#### DSPD SCE: State match program
- **Length:** 25 minutes
- **Frequency:** Once
- **Description:** Provides an overview of the state match program, including its purpose, structure, and target population. Learn the support coordinator's key roles and responsibilities, how to navigate funding, billing, and WHX code requirements, and how to manage case transitions and closures effectively.
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

#### DSPD SCE: Challenging behaviors (Open Future Learning)
- **Length:** 3 hours 45 minutes
- **Frequency:** Once
- **Description:** Explores the meaning and causes of challenging behavior and how to respond in ways that promote understanding and support. Learn how individual and environmental factors influence behavior, and how communication difficulties can play a role.
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

#### Disability conditions (Open Future Learning) - Choose ONE
- **Length:** ~2 hours each
- **Frequency:** Once
- **Description:** These courses are designed to broaden your knowledge of disability and disability conditions. Support coordinators are required to complete **one** of the following:
  - DSPD SCE: Autism
  - DSPD SCE: Dementia strategies
  - DSPD SCE: Down syndrome
  - DSPD SCE: Epilepsy
  - DSPD SCE: Fetal Alcohol Spectrum Disorder - Supporting Success
  - DSPD SCE: Mental health
  - DSPD SCE: Prader-Willi Syndrome
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

#### DSPD SCE: Disability 101
- **Length:** 30 minutes
- **Frequency:** Once
- **Description:** Introduces the concept of disability, exploring different types of disabilities and how they are perceived. Learn effective communication strategies, including the use of disability related language, and gain an understanding of the history of disability rights in the United States.
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

#### DSPD SCE: Incident/fatality reporting
- **Length:** 30 minutes
- **Frequency:** Every year
- **Description:** Provides an overview of reporting requirements, including what must be reported, to whom, and within what timeframes. Learn how to complete required reporting, become familiar with the state and federal codes and division policies that authorize these requirements.
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

#### DSPD SCE: Office of Services Review
- **Length:** 20 minutes
- **Frequency:** Every year
- **Description:** Provides an overview of the Office of Service Review's responsibilities within the support coordination contract. Learn about types of contract monitoring and when it is appropriate to contact the Office of Services Review.
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

#### DSPD SCE: Person-centered approaches, thinking, and planning (Open Future Learning)
- **Length:** 3 hours
- **Frequency:** Every other year (even fiscal years)
- **Description:** Explores the foundations of person-centered approaches, thinking, and planning, with a focus on the key principles that guide effective practice. Learn how to apply person-centered thinking tools in your work and understand what makes a planning meeting successful.
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

#### DSPD SCE: Person-centered planning (Utah specific)
- **Length:** 45 minutes
- **Frequency:** Every other year (odd fiscal years)
- **Description:** Gives learners an understanding of the key principles and best practices that underpin person-centered planning.
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

#### DSPD SCE: Self-administered (SAS) and agency services
- **Length:** 30 minutes
- **Frequency:** Once
- **Description:** Introduces the SAS service delivery model, outlining the roles and responsibilities of those involved. Learn how to integrate SAS services into person-centered planning and support coordination activities.
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

#### DSPD SCE: Settings Rule and monitoring
- **Length:** 30 minutes
- **Frequency:** Every year
- **Description:** Introduces the purpose of the Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS) Settings Rule, the rights it protects, and what those rights look like in practice. Learn how to identify and report potential rule violations and gain an understanding of what constitutes a rights restriction.
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

### Additional Required Training

- **Deadline:** 30 days (shorter than other trainings)
- **Description:** Required annual training for all Medicaid providers contracted with DSPD, including all support coordinators.
- **Access:** Assigned automatically through ULP

## Accessing Trainings in the ULP

1. **Email Notification:** The ULP will automatically notify you by email when a training has been assigned. The email includes the training name and a link to access it.

2. **Click the Link:** Click on the link in the email to access the training, or log in to your Utah Learning Portal account if you have already created one.

3. **Enroll:** Click on the "Enroll" link in the portal to access and complete the training.

4. **Completion:** Once you have completed the training, you will receive an email notification confirming completion. The ULP will also confirm completion and give you the option to print a certificate of completion.

5. **Homepage:** Your ULP homepage shows a list of trainings assigned to you (highlighted in red) and trainings you have completed (highlighted in blue). You can access assigned trainings or revisit completed trainings from this page.

## Changing Your Password in the ULP

1. On your Learning Portal Home page, click the **person icon** on the right side
2. Click **"Change Password"** in the drop-down menu
3. Enter your current (pre-assigned) password
4. Enter a new password twice
5. Click **SAVE**
6. To return to your home screen, click the **three lines (menu)** in the top left corner, then click **"Home"**

## Tracking Your Training Progress

### In USTEPS

You can find a list of completed courses in the USTEPS report portal:

1. Go to **Report > Support Coordination > Support Coordinator Core Training**
2. The report displays your most recent course completion date
3. For required courses, the cell will display in **red** if the course is not completed
4. The completion date will be **highlighted** when there are less than 30 days before the next course completion is due

### In the CREJ Portal

Use the CREJ Portal training tracking system to:
- View all required trainings for your role
- Mark trainings as complete
- Track completion dates
- View upcoming deadlines

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can I contact if I have problems accessing or navigating trainings?

Contact **dspdlearninginfo@utah.gov** for assistance with any training issues or questions.

### How will I know when a training is assigned or due?

The ULP will send you email notifications when a training has been assigned. With the exception of Medicaid 101 (30-day requirement), support coordinators have 60 days to complete each assigned training. You can also log in to your ULP account to see a list of assigned and completed trainings.

### Why do some trainings in the ULP look different from others?

Most required support coordinator core trainings were created in-house by the division. Some trainings were created by third-party vendors (like Open Future Learning) to supplement training offerings. While all trainings are hosted in the ULP, the look and navigation may vary slightly.

### Are there other trainings I need to complete that are not part of the required core training?

Yes. While not part of the required support coordinator core training, the **Medicaid 101 training** is a required annual training for all Medicaid providers contracted with DSPD, including all support coordinators. This will automatically be assigned to you every year through the ULP.

### What if I miss the required deadline?

The report in the USTEPS report portal will show the assignment deadline and the actual completion date. These records may be used by the Office of Services Review to monitor contract compliance.
