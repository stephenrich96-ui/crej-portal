# DSPD Client Service Procedures - Support Coordinator Guide

## 🎯 Quick Reference: Common Client Situations

### ⚡ Emergency/Crisis Situations

**If client is in immediate danger:**
1. Call 911 immediately
2. Notify manager within 15 minutes
3. Document incident in Health E within 24 hours
4. Follow up with client/family within 48 hours
5. Complete incident report form

**If client needs urgent service changes:**
1. Assess situation immediately
2. Contact service providers same day
3. Update service plan if needed
4. Document all actions taken
5. Notify manager

## 📋 Client Intake Process (New Client)

### Step 1: Initial Contact (Day 1)
- [ ] Receive referral from DSPD
- [ ] Review referral information
- [ ] Contact client/family within 2 business days
- [ ] Introduce yourself and explain role
- [ ] Schedule initial assessment meeting
- [ ] Document contact in Health E

### Step 2: Initial Assessment (Week 1)
- [ ] **Prepare for Meeting**
  - [ ] Review referral information
  - [ ] Prepare assessment questions
  - [ ] Gather necessary forms
  - [ ] Confirm meeting location/time

- [ ] **Conduct Assessment**
  - [ ] Meet with client and family/guardian
  - [ ] Complete comprehensive assessment
  - [ ] Identify strengths and needs
  - [ ] Discuss goals and preferences
  - [ ] Review available services
  - [ ] Answer questions

- [ ] **Documentation**
  - [ ] Complete assessment form (Form 0-0)
  - [ ] Document meeting notes
  - [ ] Upload to Health E within 3 business days
  - [ ] Notify manager of completion

### Step 3: Service Planning (Week 2)
- [ ] **Develop Service Plan**
  - [ ] Review assessment findings
  - [ ] Identify service needs
  - [ ] Research available providers
  - [ ] Develop person-centered goals
  - [ ] Create service plan (Form 0-2)

- [ ] **Review with Client**
  - [ ] Present service plan
  - [ ] Get client/family input
  - [ ] Make revisions as needed
  - [ ] Obtain signatures/approval

- [ ] **Submit for Approval**
  - [ ] Complete all required forms
  - [ ] Submit to DSPD via Health E
  - [ ] Follow up on approval status
  - [ ] Document submission date

### Step 4: Service Implementation (Week 3-4)
- [ ] **Coordinate Services**
  - [ ] Contact service providers
  - [ ] Verify provider availability
  - [ ] Coordinate start dates
  - [ ] Ensure services begin as planned

- [ ] **Initial Monitoring**
  - [ ] Check in with client after 1 week
  - [ ] Verify services started
  - [ ] Address any concerns
  - [ ] Document initial monitoring

## 🔄 Ongoing Service Coordination

### Monthly Tasks (Every Client)

**First Week of Month:**
- [ ] **Client Contact** (Required monthly minimum)
  - [ ] Contact client or family
  - [ ] Assess current situation
  - [ ] Identify any concerns or changes
  - [ ] Document contact in Health E

- [ ] **Service Monitoring**
  - [ ] Verify services being provided
  - [ ] Check service quality
  - [ ] Address any service issues
  - [ ] Document monitoring activities

**Throughout Month:**
- [ ] **Provider Communication**
  - [ ] Respond to provider inquiries
  - [ ] Coordinate service changes
  - [ ] Address billing questions
  - [ ] Document all communications

- [ ] **Documentation**
  - [ ] Complete progress notes
  - [ ] Update service plan as needed
  - [ ] File all documents in Health E
  - [ ] Maintain organized case file

### Quarterly Tasks
- [ ] **Service Plan Review**
  - [ ] Review current service plan
  - [ ] Assess progress toward goals
  - [ ] Identify needed changes
  - [ ] Update plan if necessary

- [ ] **Stakeholder Meeting** (if needed)
  - [ ] Schedule with client/family
  - [ ] Invite service providers
  - [ ] Review progress and goals
  - [ ] Update service plan

## 📅 Annual Review Process

### 60 Days Before Annual Review Date

**Preparation:**
- [ ] **Schedule Assessment**
  - [ ] Contact client/family
  - [ ] Schedule comprehensive assessment
  - [ ] Notify service providers
  - [ ] Prepare assessment materials

- [ ] **Gather Information**
  - [ ] Review past year's progress
  - [ ] Collect provider reports
  - [ ] Review service utilization
  - [ ] Identify changes in needs

### 30 Days Before Annual Review Date

**Assessment:**
- [ ] **Conduct Comprehensive Assessment**
  - [ ] Meet with client and family
  - [ ] Complete assessment forms
  - [ ] Review all services
  - [ ] Assess goal achievement
  - [ ] Identify new needs

- [ ] **Service Plan Development**
  - [ ] Update goals based on assessment
  - [ ] Revise services as needed
  - [ ] Complete new service plan (Form 0-2)
  - [ ] Review with client/family

### On or Before Annual Review Date

**Submission:**
- [ ] **Complete All Forms**
  - [ ] Updated service plan
  - [ ] Assessment documentation
  - [ ] Required attachments
  - [ ] All signatures obtained

- [ ] **Submit to DSPD**
  - [ ] Upload to Health E
  - [ ] Verify submission successful
  - [ ] Document submission date
  - [ ] Follow up on approval

## 🆘 Common Client Questions & Answers

### "How do I change my services?"
1. Discuss desired changes with coordinator
2. Coordinator assesses if change is appropriate
3. If approved, coordinator updates service plan
4. Coordinator submits change to DSPD
5. Wait for approval before services change

**Training Link**: [Link to service plan modification section in DSPD manual]

### "What if I'm not happy with my provider?"
1. Discuss concerns with coordinator immediately
2. Coordinator will investigate and address
3. May involve provider meeting or service change
4. Coordinator documents resolution
5. If needed, coordinator can help find new provider

**Training Link**: [Link to provider management section]

### "When is my annual review?"
- Check Health E for exact date
- Coordinator will contact you 60 days before
- Annual reviews must be completed on time
- Contact coordinator if you have questions

**Training Link**: [Link to annual review procedures]

### "What services am I eligible for?"
- Eligibility determined by DSPD
- Services based on assessed needs
- Coordinator can explain available options
- Service plan lists approved services

**Training Link**: [Link to waiver services overview]

### "How do I file a complaint?"
1. Can discuss with coordinator first
2. Can contact DSPD directly
3. Coordinator can provide complaint process information
4. All complaints taken seriously
