# DSPD Support Coordinator Start Here

Welcome to CREJ! This guide will walk you through everything you need to get started as a DSPD Support Coordinator. Follow these steps in order to complete your onboarding.

## Step 1: Complete Access Forms

Before you can access any systems, you need to complete and submit the required access forms.

### Forms to Complete:

1. **[0-2 DHHS Private Support Coordinator USTEPS Access Form.pdf](/Desktop/CREJ/DSPD%20files%20needed%20to%20be%20sent/0-2%20DHHS%20Private%20Support%20Coordiantor%20USTEPS%20Access%20Form.pdf)**
   - Download and fill out completely
   - Use your CREJ email address

2. **[0-8 UPI ACCESS Form.pdf](/Desktop/CREJ/DSPD%20files%20needed%20to%20be%20sent/0-8%20UPI%20ACCESS%20Form.pdf)**
   - Download and fill out completely
   - Use your CREJ email address

### Submit Forms:

- **Email both completed forms to:** usteps@utah.gov
- **Use your CREJ email address** when submitting
- Keep copies for your records

### Create Utah ID Account:

If you don't already have one, create a Utah ID account: [Click Here](https://id.utah.gov/signup)

**Important:** Use the same email address for:
- USTEPS forms
- Utah ID account
- ULP (Utah Learning Portal)
- SELN training registration

---

## Step 2: Access the Utah Learning Portal (ULP)

The Utah Learning Portal (ULP) is where you'll complete most of your required trainings.

### Access the Portal:

1. Go to: [Utah Learning Portal](https://utahlearningportal.com)
2. Your username is your **email address** on file with DSPD
3. ULP will send you a **default password** for your first login
4. **Change your password** after first login (see instructions below)

### Change Your Password:

1. Log into ULP
2. Click the **person icon** in the top right
3. Select **"Change Password"**
4. Enter your current password
5. Enter your new password twice
6. Click **SAVE**

---

## Step 3: Complete Required Trainings (Initial Assignments)

You have **60 days** to complete these initial required trainings. The ULP will automatically assign them and send you email notifications.

### Training Access:

- Trainings are **automatically assigned** through the ULP
- You'll receive **email notifications** when trainings are assigned
- Click the link in the email to access each training
- Or log into ULP and see assigned trainings on your homepage

### Initial Required Trainings (Complete within 60 days):

1. **DSPD SCE: Acquiring and maintaining integrated community-based housing**
   - Duration: 25 minutes
   - Frequency: Every year
   - Access: [Utah Learning Portal](https://utahlearningportal.com)

2. **DSPD SCE: Profound and complex disabilities (Open Future Learning)**
   - Duration: 2 hours 35 minutes
   - Frequency: Once
   - Access: [Utah Learning Portal](https://utahlearningportal.com)

3. **DSPD SCE: Ethics training for support coordinators**
   - Duration: 25 minutes
   - Frequency: Every year
   - Access: [Utah Learning Portal](https://utahlearningportal.com)

4. **DSPD SCE: Finance**
   - Duration: 40 minutes
   - Frequency: Every year
   - Access: [Utah Learning Portal](https://utahlearningportal.com)

5. **DSPD SCE: Records management**
   - Duration: 20 minutes
   - Frequency: Every year
   - Access: [Utah Learning Portal](https://utahlearningportal.com)

6. **DSPD SCE: Health monitoring: The fatal five (Open Future Learning)**
   - Duration: 2 hours
   - Frequency: Once
   - Access: [Utah Learning Portal](https://utahlearningportal.com)

## Step 4: Register for SELN Training (Due by June 5, 2026)

**Supporting a vision for employment** is a required training that you must register for manually. It is NOT automatically assigned in the ULP.

### Registration Details:

- **Training:** Supporting a vision for employment
- **Duration:** 5 hours 30 minutes
- **Frequency:** Once
- **Deadline:** Must be completed by June 5, 2026
- **Registration:** [Click Here to Register](https://seln.org/training/supporting-a-vision-for-employment)

### Important Registration Notes:

- **Use the same email address** that you use for USTEPS and ULP
- Registration opens **one week before** the course start date
- Registration closes **one week before** the course ends
- If you've already completed this training, you don't need to retake it

### Remaining Course Offerings:

- **WINTER SECTION:** January 5 – February 13, 2026
- **SPRING SECTION:** March 2 – April 10, 2026
- **SUMMER SECTION:** April 27 – June 5, 2026

**Action Required:** Register for one of these sections as soon as possible to ensure you complete by the deadline.

## Step 8: Track Your Training Progress

### In USTEPS:

1. Go to **Report > Support Coordination > Support Coordinator Core Training**
2. View your training completion status
3. Red cells = Not completed
4. Highlighted dates = Less than 30 days until next completion due

### In CREJ Portal:

1. Go to **Trainings** section
2. View all required trainings for your role
3. Mark trainings as complete when finished
4. Track completion dates and deadlines

---

## Training Completion Tips

1. **Check Email Regularly:** ULP sends email notifications when trainings are assigned
2. **Complete Early:** Don't wait until the deadline - complete trainings as soon as they're assigned
3. **Save Certificates:** Print or save completion certificates from ULP
4. **Track in CREJ Portal:** Mark trainings complete in the CREJ Portal to track your progress
5. **Ask Questions:** Contact dspdlearninginfo@utah.gov if you have training questions
