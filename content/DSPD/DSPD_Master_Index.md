## 📋 Common Workflows

### New Client Intake
1. Refer to: `DSPD_Client_Service_Procedures.md` → Client Intake Process
2. Use: Form 0-0 (Initial Service Option)
3. Training: Week 3 - Initial Client Intake
4. Support: Manager or experienced coordinator

### Service Plan Creation
1. Refer to: `DSPD_Client_Service_Procedures.md` → Service Planning
2. Use: Form 0-2 (Service Plan)
3. Training: Week 2 - Service Planning Process
4. Resource: `DSPD_ISO_Form_Guidance.md`

### Annual Review
1. Refer to: `DSPD_Client_Service_Procedures.md` → Annual Review Process
2. Timeline: Start 60 days before due date
3. Training: Week 3 - Annual Reviews
4. Forms: Assessment + Updated Service Plan

### Training Question
1. Check: `DSPD_Training_Resource_Library.md`
2. Find topic and click training link
3. Review relevant section in DSPD Manual
4. Ask manager if still unclear

## 🎓 Training Philosophy

**Goal**: Support coordinators should be able to do their job WITHOUT having to complete full trainings. They should have:
- Clear, simple procedures to follow
- Quick access to training resources when questions arise
- Links to specific manual sections for detailed information
- Manager support when needed

**Approach**: 
- Procedures first (what to do)
- Training links second (why/how)
- Manager support always available
