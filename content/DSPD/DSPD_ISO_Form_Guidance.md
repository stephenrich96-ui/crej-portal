## ❓ ISO Form: "Brief Description of Intended Program and Program Size"

### What to Include:

**Brief Description of Intended Program:**
> "CREJ provides support coordination services for individuals with developmental disabilities through the DSPD waiver program. Services include comprehensive assessment, person-centered service planning, coordination of community supports, and ongoing monitoring to ensure quality care and compliance with waiver requirements."

**Program Size:**
> "Currently serving [X] individuals with a capacity of [Y]. Staffed by [Z] support coordinators across [number] service regions. Anticipated growth to [future capacity] within [timeframe]."

### Template for Your Agency:

**Brief Description:**
- Purpose of your program (support coordination)
- Target population (individuals with developmental disabilities)
- Key services provided (assessment, planning, coordination, monitoring)
- Compliance focus (waiver requirements)

**Program Size:**
- Current number of individuals served
- Maximum capacity
- Number of staff/coordinators
- Geographic coverage (if applicable)
- Growth projections (if applicable)

### Where to Find This Information:
- Check with manager for exact numbers
- Review agency capacity planning documents
- Update quarterly or as capacity changes

## 📝 Form Completion Tips

### General Guidelines:
1. **Read the form completely** before starting
2. **Gather all required information** before filling out
3. **Use clear, professional language**
4. **Be specific and detailed** - avoid vague statements
5. **Check for completeness** before submission
6. **Review with manager** before submitting to DSPD

### Common Mistakes to Avoid:
- ❌ Leaving sections blank (use N/A if truly not applicable)
- ❌ Using vague language
- ❌ Missing required signatures
- ❌ Submitting without manager review
- ❌ Missing attachments or supporting documentation

### Quality Checklist:
- [ ] All required fields completed
- [ ] Information is accurate and current
- [ ] Language is clear and professional
- [ ] All signatures obtained
- [ ] Supporting documentation attached
- [ ] Reviewed by manager
- [ ] Ready for submission

## 📞 Getting Help with Forms

### If You're Stuck:
1. **Review form instructions** in DSPD manual
2. **Check training materials** from Week 2
3. **Look at completed examples** (ask manager)
4. **Ask your manager** - they can review and provide guidance
5. **Contact experienced coordinator** for peer support

### Remember:
- Forms must be accurate and complete
- Manager review is required before submission
- When in doubt, ask - don't guess
- Practice with sample cases during training
