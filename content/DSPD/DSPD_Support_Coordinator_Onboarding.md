# DSPD Support Coordinator Onboarding Checklist

## 📋 Pre-Start Checklist (Manager Completes)

### Before First Day
- [ ] Complete background check and clearance
- [ ] Verify credentials and certifications
- [ ] Set up email account and system access
- [ ] Prepare workspace and equipment
- [ ] Schedule orientation meeting

### System Access Setup
- [ ] Health E (DSPD system) account created
- [ ] MyCase access configured
- [ ] Email account active
- [ ] Phone/voicemail set up
- [ ] Office keys and access cards issued

## 🎓 Training Requirements (Complete in Order)

### Week 1: Foundation Training
- [ ] **DSPD Manual Overview** - Review Introduction to DSPD Applications
  - Link: https://dspdmanuals.hs.utah.gov/#t=Introduction_to_DSPD_Applications.htm
  - Time: 2 hours
  - Quiz: Complete understanding check

- [ ] **Support Coordinator Role & Responsibilities**
  - Review job description and expectations
  - Understand scope of practice
  - Review code of ethics
  - Time: 1 hour

- [ ] **DSPD Waiver Programs Overview**
  - Community Supports Waiver
  - Community Transitions Waiver
  - Limited Supports Waiver
  - Physical Disabilities Waiver
  - Acquired Brain Injury Waiver
  - Training videos: `/Desktop/CREJ/DSPD Waiver Videos/`
  - Time: 3 hours

- [ ] **Health E System Training**
  - Account navigation
  - Client search and access
  - Document upload procedures
  - Form completion basics
  - Time: 2 hours
  - Practice: Complete 3 sample entries

### Week 2: Core Procedures
- [ ] **ISO Forms Training**
  - Form 0-0: Initial Service Option
  - Form 0-2: Service Plan
  - Form 0-5: [Review form purpose]
  - Form 0-8: [Review form purpose]
  - Form 0-9: [Review form purpose]
  - Location: `/Desktop/CREJ/DSPD files needed to be sent/`
  - Time: 4 hours
  - Practice: Complete sample forms with manager review

- [ ] **Service Planning Process**
  - Person-centered planning principles
  - Assessment procedures
  - Goal setting and documentation
  - Service coordination requirements
  - Time: 3 hours

- [ ] **Documentation Standards**
  - Progress note requirements
  - Timeliness standards
  - Quality expectations
  - Audit preparation
  - Time: 2 hours

### Week 3: Client Management
- [ ] **Initial Client Intake Process**
  - Referral procedures
  - Initial contact protocols
  - Assessment scheduling
  - Documentation requirements
  - Time: 2 hours

- [ ] **Ongoing Service Coordination**
  - Regular contact requirements
  - Service monitoring procedures
  - Provider communication
  - Crisis response protocols
  - Time: 3 hours

- [ ] **Annual Reviews & Reassessments**
  - Timeline requirements
  - Documentation needed
  - Stakeholder involvement
  - Plan updates
  - Time: 2 hours

### Week 4: Advanced Topics
- [ ] **Compliance & Quality Assurance**
  - DSPD compliance requirements
  - Quality measures
  - Corrective action procedures
  - Time: 2 hours

- [ ] **Crisis Management**
  - Emergency procedures
  - Reporting requirements
  - Documentation standards
  - Time: 1.5 hours

- [ ] **Shadowing & Observation**
  - Shadow experienced coordinator (minimum 3 client visits)
  - Observe service planning meetings
  - Review completed case files
  - Time: 8 hours

## 🎯 First 30 Days Goals

### Week 1-2
- Complete all foundation training
- Shadow experienced coordinator
- Review 5 completed case files

### Week 3-4
- Begin managing 2-3 clients (with supervision)
- Complete first service plan independently
- Attend team meetings

### Month 2-3
- Gradually increase caseload
- Complete first annual review
- Demonstrate competency in all core procedures
