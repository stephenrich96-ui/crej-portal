# DSPD Training Resource Library
## Quick Access Guide for Support Coordinators

### 🎯 How to Use This Guide
When you have a question, find the topic below and click the link to the relevant training section. No need to read everything - just find what you need!

---

## 📚 Core Training Topics

### 1. Introduction & Overview
**When to use**: First week, understanding your role
- **DSPD Manual**: https://dspdmanuals.hs.utah.gov/#t=Introduction_to_DSPD_Applications.htm
- **Training Video**: DSPD Overview (if available)
- **Time**: 2 hours
- **Key Points**: What DSPD is, your role, basic procedures

### 2. Waiver Programs
**When to use**: Understanding different waiver types
- **Training Videos**: 
  - Community Supports Waiver: `/Desktop/CREJ/DSPD Waiver Videos/Community Supports Waiver.mp4`
  - Community Transitions Waiver: `/Desktop/CREJ/DSPD Waiver Videos/Community Transitions Waiver.mp4`
  - Limited Supports Waiver: `/Desktop/CREJ/DSPD Waiver Videos/Limited Supports Waiver.mp4`
  - Physical Disabilities Waiver: `/Desktop/CREJ/DSPD Waiver Videos/Physical Disabiliteis Waiver.mp4`
  - Acquired Brain Injury: `/Desktop/CREJ/DSPD Waiver Videos/Aquired Brain Injury .mp4`
- **DSPD Manual**: Waiver Programs Section
- **Time**: 3 hours total
- **Key Points**: Differences between waivers, eligibility, services available

### 3. ISO Forms
**When to use**: Completing any DSPD form
- **Form Location**: `/Desktop/CREJ/DSPD files needed to be sent/`
- **Forms Available**:
  - Form 0-0: Initial Service Option
  - Form 0-2: Service Plan
  - Form 0-5: [Purpose]
  - Form 0-8: [Purpose]
  - Form 0-9: [Purpose]
- **DSPD Manual**: ISO Forms Section
- **Training**: Form completion training (Week 2)
- **Key Points**: When to use each form, how to complete, submission process

### 4. Service Planning
**When to use**: Creating or updating service plans
- **DSPD Manual**: Service Planning Section
- **Training Video**: Service Planning Process (if available)
- **Form**: Form 0-2 (Service Plan)
- **Time**: 3 hours training + ongoing practice
- **Key Points**: Person-centered planning, goal setting, service selection

### 5. Client Assessment
**When to use**: Initial intake or annual reviews
- **DSPD Manual**: Assessment Procedures
- **Form**: Form 0-0 (Initial Service Option)
- **Time**: 2 hours training
- **Key Points**: Comprehensive assessment, identifying needs, documenting findings

### 6. Documentation Standards
**When to use**: Writing progress notes, documenting contacts
- **DSPD Manual**: Documentation Requirements
- **Health E Guide**: Documentation section
- **Time**: 2 hours training
- **Key Points**: Timeliness, completeness, quality standards

### 7. Annual Reviews
**When to use**: 60 days before annual review date
- **DSPD Manual**: Annual Review Procedures
- **Training**: Annual Review Process (Week 3)
- **Forms**: Assessment + Updated Service Plan
- **Time**: 2 hours training
- **Key Points**: Timeline, required documentation, submission process

### 8. Service Coordination
**When to use**: Ongoing client management
- **DSPD Manual**: Service Coordination Section
- **Training**: Ongoing Service Coordination (Week 3)
- **Time**: 3 hours training
- **Key Points**: Monthly contacts, service monitoring, provider communication

### 9. Compliance & Quality
**When to use**: Understanding requirements, preparing for audits
- **DSPD Manual**: Compliance Section
- **Training**: Compliance & Quality Assurance (Week 4)
- **Time**: 2 hours training
- **Key Points**: Requirements, quality measures, audit preparation

### 10. Crisis Management
**When to use**: Emergency situations, client safety concerns
- **DSPD Manual**: Crisis Procedures
- **Training**: Crisis Management (Week 4)
- **Protocol**: Crisis Response Protocol (separate document)
- **Time**: 1.5 hours training
- **Key Points**: Emergency response, reporting, documentation

## 🔍 Common Questions & Where to Find Answers

### "How do I complete Form 0-2?"
→ **Training**: ISO Forms Training (Week 2)
→ **Resource**: Form 0-2 in `/Desktop/CREJ/DSPD files needed to be sent/`
→ **Manual**: Service Plan Section
→ **Support**: Ask manager or experienced coordinator

### "What's the difference between the waivers?"
→ **Training**: Waiver Programs Overview (Week 1)
→ **Videos**: `/Desktop/CREJ/DSPD Waiver Videos/`
→ **Manual**: Waiver Programs Section

### "When is documentation due?"
→ **Training**: Documentation Standards (Week 2)
→ **Manual**: Documentation Requirements
→ **Rule**: Within 3 business days for most documentation

### "How do I do an annual review?"
→ **Training**: Annual Reviews (Week 3)
→ **Manual**: Annual Review Procedures
→ **Guide**: See "Client Service Procedures" document

### "What if a client needs service changes?"
→ **Training**: Service Planning (Week 2)
→ **Manual**: Service Plan Modification
→ **Process**: Update service plan, submit to DSPD

### "What do I do in an emergency?"
→ **Training**: Crisis Management (Week 4)
→ **Protocol**: Crisis Response Protocol
→ **Action**: Call 911 if immediate danger, notify manager

### "How do I use Health E?"
→ **Training**: Health E System Training (Week 1)
→ **Support**: IT or manager
→ **Practice**: Complete sample entries during training

### "What services are available?"
→ **Training**: Waiver Programs Overview (Week 1)
→ **Manual**: Available Services Section
→ **Resource**: Service catalog in Health E
